## 2026-06-17 - Trilingual Interaction Design in Telegram
**Learning:** Localizing system feedback (waiting, errors, disclaimers) is essential for a consistent trilingual experience in legal bots. Additionally, editing existing status messages instead of sending new ones significantly reduces chat noise and makes the bot feel more responsive.
**Action:** Use a centralized localization mapping and `message.edit_text` for status updates in future Telegram bot projects.
