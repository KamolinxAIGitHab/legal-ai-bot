## 2024-06-15 - Trilingual Localization and Message Editing
**Learning:** Localizing system feedback (waiting, errors, disclaimers) is essential for a consistent trilingual experience in this legal bot. Additionally, editing existing status messages instead of sending new ones significantly reduces chat noise and makes the bot feel more responsive and "snappier".
**Action:** Always use a centralized localization mapping and prioritize editing "waiting" messages with the final response instead of sending new ones.
